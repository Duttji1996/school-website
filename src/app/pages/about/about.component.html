Who We Are

Usha Devi Convent School (located in Lalganj, Mirzapur) is a modern, English-medium institution opening its doors in 2027. We provide quality foundational education for Classes 1 to 5. We focus on strong academic foundations, moral values, and holistic child development.

Strictly CBSE Pattern

Please note that our curriculum will strictly adhere to the CBSE pattern. No other medium or board pattern will be applicable at our institution.

Our Vision & Mission

Our vision is to create confident, responsible, and future-ready students through innovative teaching and a nurturing environment. We aim to deliver high-quality education by combining traditional values with modern technology, emphasizing English communication and overall personality development.
