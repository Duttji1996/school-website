Our Facilities

Academics & Campus

A world-class infrastructure designed specifically for young learners.

Smart Classrooms

Every classroom at UDCS is equipped with interactive digital boards and high-speed internet, making learning an immersive experience. Our tech-driven approach helps children under 10 grasp complex concepts with ease and joy.

Sports Arena

Our expansive, professional-grade arena is where students develop physical strength and teamwork. We focus on daily physical activities to ensure a healthy balance between academics and play.

Innovation Lab

Our state-of-the-art innovation lab introduces young minds to the digital and scientific world. From early coding to hands-on experiments, we ensure our students are well-prepared for a tech-driven future.

Our Curriculum

A balanced approach to learning that fosters intellectual and emotional growth.

Early Years (LKG - UKG)

Focus on play-based learning, motor skill development, and basic literacy and numeracy.

  • Phonics & Storytelling
  • Creative Arts & Crafts
  • Sensory Play
Primary (Class 1 - 3)

Building foundational knowledge in core subjects while encouraging curiosity and inquiry.

  • Environmental Studies
  • Logical Reasoning
  • Multi-lingual Literacy
Upper Primary (Class 4 - 5)

Transitioning to deeper conceptual understanding and independent learning habits.

  • Advanced Mathematics
  • Scientific Exploration
  • ICT & Digital Literacy
