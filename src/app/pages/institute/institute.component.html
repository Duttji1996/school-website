Our Legacy & History

Established with a vision to revolutionize rural education, Usha Devi Convent School (UDCS) was founded in 2026 to bridge the gap between traditional values and modern technovation.

Recognizing the potential in the youth of Mirzapur, our founders envisioned an institution where technology like AI and Smart-Learning are not luxuries, but fundamental tools for every child. Over the years, UDCS has evolved from a local primary center into a beacon of "Smart Education."
